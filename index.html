The problem

Important context gets buried

How it works

One badge, four jobs

Live in v0.1

Context Meter

Live estimates of message count, token usage, and context window pressure, right on the page.

Live in v0.1

Context Health Score

A single 0–100 risk score. Token pressure is live today; instruction decay, topic drift, and contradiction risk are landing in upcoming versions.

Coming soon

New Session Alerts

A quiet nudge once risk crosses into High Risk — not a popup for every message.

Coming soon

Context Handoff

Generates a continuation prompt — goals, decisions, and next steps — so you can carry the important parts into a fresh chat.

The score

Read it at a glance

0–50 Good

Plenty of room left.

51–70 Moderate

Getting long — worth keeping an eye on.

71–85 High Risk

Older instructions and context are under real pressure.

86–100 New Session Recommended

Time to start fresh and carry the important parts over.

Privacy

Built to never see your conversation

Everything runs on your device

Parsing, scoring, and estimates all happen locally in the browser — nothing about your conversation is sent anywhere.

No account, no server

ContextVitals has no backend that your chats pass through.

Every number says "estimated"

ContextVitals can't see a model's real internal context state, so it never claims to — only ever estimates.
